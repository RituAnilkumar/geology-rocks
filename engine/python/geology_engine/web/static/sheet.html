Geology Rocks — testing the rock key

Thank you for helping! We are testing the key, not you. A wrong result tells us what to fix, so please answer honestly and never guess.

1. Before each rock

  1. On the right of the screen, under Specimen test, type the specimen number on the tag.
  2. Type your first name or initials and choose your experience. These are remembered for your next rock.
  3. Click My kit (top right) and tick only the tools you actually have at the table.
  4. Leave Where did you find it? and Mode as they are, unless the session lead says otherwise.

2. Answering the questions

  • The suggested question is in the middle of the screen. Click the option that best matches your rock. Grey boxes are where pictures will go later; read the words.
  • Not sure: press it whenever you can't decide. This is useful information — don't guess.
  • Skip: press it if you can't do that test (for example, you don't have the tool).
  • Some questions let you tick several options: tick all you can see, then press Done.
  • You can jump to any question in the list on the left, and change or remove answers on the right.

3. When to stop

  • Stop when the key says No more useful questions, or when you are happy with its result.
  • Please don't look at the specimen's label or ask others until you have saved the test.

4. Saving

  1. Press Save this test.
  2. Say how sure you are that the key's answer is right — your gut feeling is fine.
  3. Add a note if anything was odd (a confusing question, a weathered surface…), then you're done.
  4. “Saved ✓” appears and the key starts again for your next rock.
⚠ Safety and care of specimens
  • Only scratch, use acid on, or hit a specimen if the session lead says it is allowed, and then only on a small corner. Collections are precious.
  • Dilute acid: wear eye protection, use one drop, wipe it off and wash your hands afterwards.
  • Scratch tests: rest the glass plate flat on the table, never in your hand, and push away from you.
  • Hammer: safety glasses on, and warn people near you.
Stuck? Ask the session lead. If the screen shows a red message or stops responding, tell them which specimen you were on — nothing you do can break the key.
